# Your smart screen

A 4-inch touch screen with a small computer inside: a weather station, a clock, a photo frame
and a few games. It works on its own, with no PC.

## 1. Switching it on

Plug it with the USB-C cable into a phone charger (5 V, at least 1 A). It has no power button:
it starts as soon as it gets power, and stops when unplugged.

At power-on the name of the app about to start shows for two seconds (Forecast, at first), then
the app.

## 2. Back to the menu

From any app: **drag a finger down from the top edge**. A **home button** shows at the top
centre: touch it, and after a few seconds the menu of the apps opens.

The menu also has a **Shell** button: a black screen with text, for those who want to type
commands. It is not needed: if you end up there by mistake, touch the screen to go back to the
menu.

## 3. The first time: language and WiFi

Open **Settings**. Its first page says what is set now: the WiFi, the language and the app of
power-on. At the top right are three buttons: **WiFi**, **Start** and **Display**.

- **Language.** Touch **Display**, then choose **English** or **Italiano** under **Language**.
  Apps take the language when they start: go back to the menu and open the app again.
- **WiFi.** Touch **WiFi**, then **Add** and **Scan**. Touch the name of your network, type the
  password with the keyboard and touch **Save**. Wait a few seconds: "Connected to ..." must
  show. **Back** returns to the first page, which now says that the WiFi is connected.

  The screen uses **2.4 GHz** WiFi only. If your router has two networks with similar names (for
  example one with "5G" in its name), choose the one without "5G".

- **App at power-on.** Touch **Start** and choose the app that starts by itself when the screen
  is switched on: Forecast, Clock, Photo Frame... or **Shell**: the shell shows at power-on, and a
  touch opens the menu.

## 4. Forecast

The weather now and for the next five days, for up to five places.

- **Changing a place:** touch **SET** at the top right. Touch one of the buttons **1** to **5**,
  type the name of the town, touch **Search** and then the right result in the list. Then
  **Next**, **Next** and **Save**.
- **Going from one place to another:** swipe left or right on the screen (a swipe, not a tap).
- On the second page of SET choose colours and units; on the third the brightness at night.

## 5. Clock

An analog clock with the date. The time comes from the internet, so it needs the WiFi. At night
it turns dark red and dimmer, in the same hours as set in Forecast.

## 6. Photo Frame

Shows the photos one after another. Swipe left or right to change photo; a touch shows the time
and the **Options** button.

**Putting photos on it from a phone:**

1. Touch the screen, then **Options**, then **Add photos**.
2. A QR code shows: point the phone's camera at it (the phone must be on the **same WiFi
   network**). If it does not read the code, type the address shown below it in the browser,
   and the PIN.
3. On the page that opens touch **Choose photos** and pick them from the gallery. The phone
   prepares them and sends them by itself: a few seconds per photo.
4. When you are done touch **Done** on the screen.

The same page can delete photos too. Keep it open until all the photos are "on the frame".

In **Options** you also choose how often the photo changes and the hours when the screen is off
at night (a touch turns it on for a minute).

## 7. Games

In the **Games** folder of the menu: Snake, Breakout, Pong, Simon, Tic-tac-toe, Block Drop, Star
Guard, Crossword, Frog, Orbit, Sandwich Tower and Fifteen. To leave a game: a finger down from
the top edge and the home button.

## 8. The other apps

- **EIM-01**: the control panel of an industrial machine, as a demonstration: the values are
  made up. The buttons at the bottom open its pages.
- **FTP Server**: for those who want to copy files between a computer and the screen. It shows an
  address, a user and a password to type into a program such as FileZilla, on the same WiFi.

## If something goes wrong

- **It is stuck:** unplug the cable and plug it in again.
- **An app at power-on does not work:** plug the cable in again and, while its name shows (the
  first two seconds), **touch the screen**: the shell opens; touch it again for the menu, then
  change the app in **Settings → Start**.
- **Forecast or Clock without data:** check the WiFi in **Settings**.
- **The memory card** (the SD at the back) holds the apps and the photos: do not take it out
  while the screen is on.
